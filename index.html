Jonathan & Oum
Meet the Owners

Jonathan & Oum

We're Jonathan and Oum — a husband-and-wife team from Australia and Thailand. With backgrounds in IT and farming, we never expected to end up in property, yet together we've built a portfolio that speaks for itself.

Starting 17 years ago with Happy Wife Happy Life LTD, we began small — buying land, building, selling, and reinvesting — until we could create the villas we'd always envisioned.

Blending Jonathan's structural insight with Oum's creative flair, we turn bold ideas into beautiful, liveable spaces. Our success comes from our partnership, passion, and love for what we do.
